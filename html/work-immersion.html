Since the implementation of the Senior High School (SHS) program under the K–12 curriculum in the Philippines, starting nationwide in SY 2016–2017, Culiat High School (CHS) in Tandang Sora, Quezon City, has structured its Work Immersion program around localized partnerships to transition Grade 12 students into higher education, entrepreneurship, or employment.

Key Tracks & Strands Involved

Culiat High School deploys Grade 12 students across its major SHS tracks for 80–160-hour work immersion requirements. The Academic Track includes Humanities and Social Sciences (HUMSS) and Accountancy, Business, and Management (ABM). The Technical-Vocational-Livelihood (TVL) Track includes ICT strands—CSS and Animation—and Home Economics Dressmaking.

Key Program Milestones

A program that has grown with its learners

2017–2018

First Immersion Cohort

The inaugural Grade 12 batch entered real-world deployments under DepEd Order No. 30, s. 2017 guidelines, supported by Quezon City’s city-wide SHS immersion initiative.

2020–2022

Pandemic Adaptations

With COVID-19 health protocols in place, CHS used home-based schemes, capstone projects, simulated work modules, and online skill workshops.

2023–Present

Full On-Site Deployment

Students again complete structured on-site immersion, with interviews, résumé workshops, 80–160 hours of service, portfolio creation, and culminating presentations.

CommuniCamp 2025

Bridging Understanding Through the Arts of Communication

October 23–24, 2025 CHS Covered Court

Held on October 23–24 at the CHS Covered Court, CommuniCamp 2025 gave Grade 12 learners a practical introduction to the job application process. Through student-created company booths, résumé preparation, and mock interviews, learners built the communication skills, confidence, and professionalism needed for work immersion and future careers.
